# Mulaky 🏢
### Smart Community Management for Modern Buildings

---

## What is Mulaky?

Mulaky is a web platform designed to solve the communication and management challenges that residential buildings face every day.

Buildings today struggle with:
- 📋 No centralized place to track maintenance issues
- 💸 Residents don't know where their money goes
- 🚨 No fast way to send emergency alerts to residents
- 👥 Managing hundreds of residents across multiple buildings is chaotic
- 📬 Inviting and onboarding new residents is manual and slow

**Mulaky solves all of this in one platform.**

---

## How Mulaky Works

Mulaky is built around three roles, each with their own dashboard and permissions:

---

### 👑 Admin — The Platform Manager
The Admin has full control over the entire platform.

- Adds buildings to the system
- Assigns owners to manage each building
- Views all users across the platform
- Can see live stats for every building — alerts, maintenance, residents
- Manages roles and access for all users

---

### 🏠 Owner — The Building Manager
The Owner is responsible for their building(s). An owner can manage multiple buildings, and in each building they have full control.

- Invites residents to join their building by email
- Removes residents when needed
- Publishes monthly **financial reports** so residents know exactly where their money goes — with visual charts and expense breakdowns
- Sends **alerts** to all residents instantly (emergency, warning, info, maintenance)
- Manages **maintenance requests** submitted by residents
- Posts building-wide **maintenance notices** so everyone stays informed
- Receives real-time bell notifications for new alerts

---

### 👤 Resident — The Building Member
The Resident is any person living in or associated with a building. A resident can be a member of multiple buildings simultaneously — as a resident in one and an owner in another.

- Receives an invitation from the building owner and chooses to accept or decline
- Views their building information
- Sees **financial reports** — total collected, expenses breakdown, balance
- Receives and sends **alerts** visible to all residents
- Submits **maintenance requests** — can choose to make them private (only owner sees) or public (all residents can see)
- Views maintenance notices posted by the owner
- Gets real-time bell notifications for new alerts

## Key Features

**🔔 Real-time Bell Notifications**
Every new alert instantly updates the bell icon with an unread count. Click to see all alerts in a dropdown without leaving the page.

**📊 Financial Transparency**
Owners add monthly reports with expense breakdowns. Residents view bar charts and pie charts showing exactly how building fees are distributed.

**🔧 Maintenance Tracking**
Residents submit requests with a visibility toggle — private for personal issues, public to let neighbors know about shared problems. Owners manage requests through a Kanban board with status updates.

**📬 Smart Invitation System**
Owners invite residents by email. Residents see pending invitations on their building selector page and can accept or decline with one click.

**🏢 Multi-Building Support**
A single user can be an owner of one building and a resident in another. After login, they see all their buildings and pick which one to enter.
